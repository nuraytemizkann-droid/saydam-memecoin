## Community rules

1. No price targets, coordinated buys, pump calls or “raid” pressure.
2. No harassment, hate speech, threats or doxxing.
3. No unsolicited DMs, wallet support, seed-phrase requests or lookalike links.
4. Promotions and referral links require moderator approval and disclosure.
5. Criticism and hard questions are welcome; repeated spam is not.
6. Moderators document bans and material conflicts internally.
7. Team members disclose paid relationships and token holdings when relevant.
8. Only the announcement channel, X account and canonical site may announce a contract.

## Moderator escalation

- Scam link or seed-phrase request: delete, ban, preserve evidence, warn community.
- Impersonated admin: ban and publish handle plus screenshot with personal data redacted.
- Contract exploit allegation: escalate privately to security lead, pause marketing and post a holding notice after initial verification.
- Price manipulation discussion: remove coordination content; do not debate price direction as an official account.
- Credible legal request: preserve records and route to counsel; moderators do not improvise a public legal position.
